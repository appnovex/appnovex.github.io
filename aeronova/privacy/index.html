Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains how AeroNova: Sky Pilot (“AeroNova”, “the app”), published by AppNovex (“we”, “us”), handles information. We designed AeroNova to collect as little as possible. You do not need an email address or password to play.

1. Information stored on your device

Your single-player progress (coins, unlocked aircraft, mission stars and best times, settings) is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Online play (ranked duels, weekly cup, leaderboards)

If you open the Online mode, the app creates an anonymous online account for you. We store:

  • a random account ID and, if you choose Sign in with Apple, the private identifier Apple gives us (we request no name or email);
  • the pilot name, country flag and avatar you choose (shown publicly on leaderboards);
  • your rating, league, race results, best times and flight replays (position and orientation of your aircraft during a race). Replays may be shown to other players as “ghosts”;
  • technical records used to prevent cheating (race start times, app version, rate-limit counters).

We use this data only to run online play, leaderboards and fair-play checks. We never sell it or use it for advertising. It is hosted by our processor Supabase, Inc. (servers in the EU, Ireland). You can delete your online account and all its data at any time in AeroNova › Settings › Online account › Delete online account.

4. Tracking and personalized ads

On iOS 14.5 and later, AeroNova asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is used. If you decline, ads are still shown but they are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform appears first. You can reopen it from AeroNova › Settings › Ad privacy choices.

5. Children

AeroNova is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. You can delete your online account in the app at any time. Offline progress is removed by deleting the app, and tracking and consent can be changed in the settings above. For data held by Google or Apple, use their tools linked above. You can also email us and we will help.

7. Security and retention

Online data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by row-level access rules, so each player can only reach their own private data. We keep online account data until you delete your account; inactive anonymous accounts may be removed after 24 months. Third-party partners keep data according to their own policies.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, when the change is significant, notify you in the app.
